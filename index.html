Online retail, Bulgaria

Trend wave is a small Bulgarian company preparing an online shop for customers in Bulgaria and Romania. We sell home and lifestyle products that are newly popular in Western Europe and not yet widely available here.

To choose products, we use an internal research tool, trend-radar. It reads public trend data, such as which search terms are rising, and helps us decide what to stock. The tool doesn't publish content, doesn't sign in other users, and doesn't collect personal data.
