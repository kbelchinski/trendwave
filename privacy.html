Our research tool (trend-radar)

trend-radar is an internal tool that only our own staff use. It connects to third-party services, including Pinterest, Google Trends data providers and the Meta Ad Library, using our company's own accounts. It reads public, aggregated information such as trending search terms and counts of advertisers for a product term.

  • It doesn't let other people sign in, and it doesn't ask anyone for access to their account.
  • It doesn't read, collect or store personal data about Pinterest users or any other people.
  • It doesn't post, pin or publish anything.
  • What it stores is aggregated trend data: keywords, trend values and counts. That data is kept on our own server and used only to choose products. It isn't sold or shared.
